Vision and Mission

Heritage Library Vision

Heritage Library strives to
encourage a love of reading,
empower researchers,
and guide students to excel in creativity.

Heritage Library Mission

Heritage Library’s mission is to provide resources to teachers and students
that will empower them to become a community of effective communicators,
responsible collaborators and critical thinkers in order to grow
into lifelong learners and global citizens.

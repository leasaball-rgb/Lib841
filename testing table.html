Who am I?

Meet your librarian, Leasa Ball. I am a Michigan native who has loved living in Kentucky for two-thirds of my life now.
My love of books started at a very young age where, I believe, all readers start...on the lap of a loving adult who reads aloud!
Being surrounded by books brings me great joy whether in a library, bookstore, or reading on my deck or in a hammock in the woods.
